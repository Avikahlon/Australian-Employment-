📊 Western Australia Economic Volatility Analyzer
An interactive data visualization system built with Streamlit to explore the dynamics of unemployment in Western Australia, with a focus on the impact of global economic events and the state's reliance on the mining sector.

✨ Features
This application provides a deep, interactive exploration of unemployment trends from the last two decades, emphasizing the unique economic position of Western Australia.

1. Time Series Analysis with Event Highlights
Unemployment Rate Over Time: A detailed line chart of the unemployment rate over a 20-year period, specifically highlighting trends in Western Australia (WA).

Major Event Annotations: Key global events like the Global Financial Crisis (GFC) and the COVID-19 pandemic are marked with annotations, clearly showing periods of significant unemployment spikes.

Interactive Tooltips: Hover over any point on the graph to instantly view the unemployment rate, key mining sector performance indicators, and details about the corresponding global event.

2. Sector-based Economic Analysis
Mining vs. Other Sectors: Compare unemployment rates in the mining sector directly against other key sectors within Western Australia.

Global Market Impact: Visually demonstrate how fluctuations in global commodity prices—a key driver for mining—directly impact unemployment rates in WA versus national trends.

Sector Toggle: Allows users to easily switch between different sectors for focused analysis.

3. Regional and Comparative Visualization
State-Level Heatmap: An interactive map or heatmap showing how Western Australia's unemployment rate compares to that of other Australian states.

Comparative Trends: Select different states to analyze how their unemployment trends aligned or diverged during major economic shocks like the GFC and COVID-19.

Volatility Highlighting: Provides additional context layers to illustrate Western Australia's unique volatility due to its global market dependency.

4. Volatility and Sensitivity Dashboard
Sensitivity Metrics: A dedicated dashboard that calculates and visually displays the volatility of unemployment rates in direct response to global events.

Comparative Sensitivity: Gauge how much more sensitive Western Australia's unemployment is to global market changes compared to other Australian states.

Timeframe Slider: Use interactive slider bars to focus the analysis and compare volatility metrics across specific periods (e.g., pre-GFC, GFC, post-COVID-19).

5. Historical Comparison Filters
Before & After Analysis: Specialized filters allow users to compare the immediate and long-term economic effects before and after major events.

Recovery Tracking: Visualize the spike in unemployment during these events and track the duration and speed of the subsequent economic recovery.

6. Contextual Narrative and Insights
Data Storytelling: Clear, concise narrative sections provide essential context on global events, their effect on the mining sector, and the resulting impact on overall unemployment.

Expert Insights: Data-driven summaries explaining why Western Australia's economy demonstrates higher volatility compared to other states.
